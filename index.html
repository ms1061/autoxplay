Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoX Play Launcher handles information and permissions used by the application.

Last updated: August 13, 2026

1. About AutoX Play

AutoX Play Launcher is an Android application designed to provide a convenient interface for accessing applications, navigation, multimedia and other features available on compatible devices.

AutoX Play is particularly designed for use with automotive infotainment and multimedia systems.

2. Personal Information

AutoX Play does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information for advertising purposes.

The application may use information and permissions provided by the Android operating system when necessary to provide launcher functionality.

AutoX Play does not require information such as your name, address, government identification documents, passwords, or financial information for its basic functionality.

3. Location Information

Certain navigation-related features may use location information provided by the Android operating system.

When you use a third-party navigation application through AutoX Play, such as a maps or navigation application, location information is handled according to the privacy policy of that respective application.

AutoX Play does not use location information for targeted advertising.

4. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth

Depending on the device and enabled features, AutoX Play may request access to Android connectivity features, including Wi-Fi and Bluetooth.

These permissions may be used to provide access to or control connectivity features available on the device.

AutoX Play does not use these permissions to sell or share personal information for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Applications

AutoX Play may allow you to launch and use applications installed on your device, including navigation, music, communication and other compatible applications.

These applications are developed and operated by third parties and may have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

AutoX Play does not control the privacy practices of third-party applications. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those applications before using them.

6. Local Storage

AutoX Play may store certain information locally on your device when necessary to provide its functionality.

This may include application preferences, launcher configuration, interface settings and other settings required to maintain your experience.

Such information is stored locally on the device and is not used for advertising purposes.

7. Data Sharing

AutoX Play does not sell personal information.

AutoX Play does not share personal information with third parties for behavioral advertising purposes.

Third-party services or applications used through AutoX Play may have their own data practices, which are independent of this Privacy Policy.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application against unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse.

However, no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored on a device.

9. Children's Privacy

AutoX Play is not specifically directed toward children and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, or applicable legal requirements.

When significant changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

11. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request information about its processing, access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

For privacy-related requests, please contact us using the contact information provided below.
